GPSim-lo privacy policy

App: GPSim-lo (se.louie.gpsimlo) · Publisher: TELAMON · Last updated: 13 October 2026

GPSim-lo is a GPS route simulator for testing location-based apps. It records and replays paths on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with data. In short: we do not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Paths and recordings. Recorded paths, imported GPX files, path names and usage counts are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They leave the phone only when you choose to export or share a path.
  • Settings. Stored only on your phone.
  • Location. The app reads your location only while you record a path (a notification is shown the whole time). It does not use location in the background otherwise and never sends it to us. During playback the app supplies simulated locations to your phone; it does not read your real location for that.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

Data that leaves your phone

  • Map tiles. To show maps, the app downloads map images from OpenFreeMap (or another tile server you enter in Settings). Like any web request, this reveals your IP address and which map area is being viewed to that server. The app sends no identifier, no location fix and no path data with these requests. OpenFreeMap states that it uses no cookies and has no user database.
  • Purchases. The premium unlock is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's privacy policy; the app only receives whether premium is unlocked.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate): to record a path while you walk or drive.
  • Foreground service (location) and notifications: so recording and playback keep running with the screen off, with a visible notification.
  • Internet and network state: to download map tiles.
  • Mock location (selected by you in Android's developer options): to supply simulated positions during playback.

Children

The app is a developer tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version sends any data elsewhere (for example to a road-snapping service), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
